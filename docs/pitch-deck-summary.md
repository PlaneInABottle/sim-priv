# Kozmopath — AI That Sells For You

---

## The Problem

Small and mid-size e-commerce businesses lose customers on WhatsApp every day.

- Customers message about a product and wait hours for a reply
- Support staff spend 80% of their time answering the same questions
- Custom-dimension products (screens, curtains, shutters) require manual calculation
- Human agents forget context, re-ask the same questions, frustrate customers

**The result:** missed sales, high support costs, slow growth.

---

## The Solution

Kozmopath is an AI customer support agent that lives in your WhatsApp inbox.

It reads the customer's message, pulls live products from your store, calculates prices, and sends a helpful reply — in under 5 seconds.

No waiting. No wrong prices. No re-asking.

---

## How It Works

```
Customer sends a WhatsApp message
        ↓
AI reads it and understands the intent
        ↓
AI fetches live products from your store (ikas, Shopify, etc.)
        ↓
AI calculates the right price for the customer's dimensions
        ↓
Customer gets a clean reply with product links and prices
        ↓
If it gets complex → AI hands off to a human with full context
```

> Renk tercihiniz var mı?

> **Customer:** 80x180 var mı?
>

The AI understood the dimension exceeded the window limit and automatically suggested the door product — without asking the customer to repeat themselves.

## What the AI Does Automatically

- Fetches your live product catalog on every message (always accurate, never stale)
- Calculates price for custom-dimension products (screens, curtains, shutters)
- Routes customers to the right product based on their measurements
- Remembers what was said earlier — no redundant questions
- Recognizes returning customers by phone number
- Hands off to a human sales agent with a full context note when needed
